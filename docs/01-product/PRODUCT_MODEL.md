# Product Model

Status: **Draft for human approval**

## Core product cycle

MetaEdge organizes the product around one progression:

**Discover → Understand → Test → Act → Manage → Learn → Scale**

The purpose of this model is to keep the product coherent even as the underlying sources and trading mechanisms become more advanced.

## Where an edge may come from

A user may discover an edge through:

- a market pattern;
- a wallet;
- a trader;
- a strategy;
- an agent;
- a portfolio;
- a cohort;
- a signal provider;
- a behavioral/psychology pattern;
- a catalyst or narrative;
- a cross-market relationship.

The system should not force every user to invent a strategy from scratch.

## Canonical progression

### Discover

Find something potentially interesting:

- a moving market;
- unusual flow;
- a profitable wallet;
- a trader worth studying;
- a strategy;
- an agent;
- a behavioral regime;
- an emerging catalyst.

### Understand

Explain what is happening and what we do **not** know:

- what the source is doing;
- why the market may be moving;
- which evidence agrees or conflicts;
- risk, leverage, concentration, liquidity, and timing;
- whether the observed wallet/trader may be only one leg of a larger exposure;
- how fresh and complete the evidence is.

### Test

Choose the appropriate evidence-building method:

- shadow;
- historical backtest;
- simulation;
- paper scout;
- paper copy;
- agent experiment.

### Act

Take bounded paper exposure when the opportunity is valid enough to learn from.

Action does not require certainty. Exposure should reflect evidence quality and risk limits.

### Manage

Continuously reassess the position:

- add;
- hold;
- reduce;
- exit;
- hedge;
- reverse where permitted.

### Learn

Evaluate:

- thesis quality;
- signal quality;
- market regime;
- execution assumptions;
- costs;
- sizing;
- behavioral mistakes;
- missed opportunities;
- whether abstention was beneficial or overly conservative.

### Scale

Scale may mean:

- larger paper exposure;
- broader strategy allocation;
- adding the source to a portfolio;
- Arena/competition use;
- future real proposal eligibility;
- future bounded delegated authority.

Scale is not automatic promotion.

## Candidate V1

The candidate V1 should prove the product without requiring real money.

Core V1 capabilities:

1. Discover markets / wallets / traders / strategies / agents.
2. Investigate and explain source behavior and evidence.
3. Follow a source.
4. Create or copy a strategy.
5. Backtest where appropriate.
6. Shadow a source or strategy.
7. Create a paper portfolio.
8. Paper trade or paper copy.
9. Manage positions dynamically.
10. Review outcomes and missed opportunities.
11. Improve or retire the strategy/copy relationship.

## Candidate later phases

### Phase 2

- Agent Arena;
- source/strategy comparison;
- cohort intelligence;
- richer social/copy surfaces;
- agent reputation and maturity scoring.

### Phase 3+

- real-readiness assessment;
- exact real proposals;
- supervised real execution;
- bounded autonomous execution under explicit policy;
- paper-vs-real execution comparison;
- adaptive portfolio agents.

## Primary user jobs

The product should support several jobs without turning them into separate products.

### Learner / emerging trader

"Help me understand what is happening and practice without risking capital."

### Source follower

"Find wallets, traders, strategies, or agents worth studying and let me test following them safely."

### Strategy builder

"Turn my idea into explicit rules, test it honestly, and understand whether it behaves as expected."

### Advanced experimental trader

"Run multiple disciplined experiments in parallel and help me distinguish signal, execution, regime, and luck."

## Product non-goals for the foundation phase

MetaEdge is not currently optimizing for:

- highest trade frequency;
- fully autonomous real-money trading before paper competence is demonstrated;
- one monolithic LLM deciding everything;
- a single magical confidence score;
- blind wallet cloning;
- preserving every historical feature merely because code exists.
