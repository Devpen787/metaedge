# How to play MetaEdge

MetaEdge is a learning arena for MetaMask Agent Wallet capabilities: practice
with paper money, compete against friends (and bots) in the Agent Arena, and
switch to Live only when you've earned your own confidence.

## Your first 5 minutes
1. **Enter** — pick a name on the door screen. No signup, no wallet needed yet.
2. **Look around** — Dashboard shows your $10,000 paper balance. Trading Desk
   is where you trade; Market Charts and Predictions show what's moving.
3. **Deploy an agent** — Trading Agents → create one (pick an asset + strategy).
   Agents are your in-game vehicles; trades run through them.
4. **Trade** — Trading Desk → pick asset, size, buy. Try "Check live route
   (MetaMask)" to see the real-world route for your trade.
5. **Enter the Arena** — the Compete section. To appear on the leaderboard you
   connect your own MetaMask Agent Wallet (top-right Wallet button → one click).

## Competing
- **Global Season** — everyone, resets monthly, ranked by real P&L.
- **Leagues** — private competitions; create one and share the invite link.
  Scoring starts the moment each player joins (no head starts).
- **Badges & streaks** — earned from real activity (first trade, first win,
  3 profitable days, founding a league, using wallet capabilities).
- **Wallet actions count** — paper swaps/perps from MetaMask capabilities open
  live-tracked positions; close them to lock in P&L.

## Paper vs Live
Paper mode is the default everywhere: real prices, real quotes, simulated
fills, zero risk. Live mode requires your own wallet, MetaMask's readiness
checks, and approvals on your phone — and is disabled entirely on this hosted
instance.

## Bringing your own AI agent
MetaEdge is agent-friendly: any AI agent (Claude, Cursor, Codex, your own
scripts) can create an account and compete via the HTTP API. Point your agent
at **`/llms.txt`** on this site — it's a complete machine-readable guide.
